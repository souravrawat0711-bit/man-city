Our History

1880

Manchester City Football Club was founded in 1880 as St. Mark's (West Gorton). The club was renamed Ardwick Association Football Club in 1887, before becoming Manchester City in 1894.

From humble beginnings in East Manchester, City have evolved into one of the world's most formidable clubs — a transformation accelerated by the Abu Dhabi United Group's acquisition in 2008.

Under visionary managers and world-class players, Manchester City have rewritten English football's history books, becoming the first team to win four consecutive Premier League titles.

Home Ground

Etihad Stadium

The Etihad Stadium — officially the City of Manchester Stadium — has been Manchester City's fortress since 2003. Built for the 2002 Commonwealth Games, it stands as one of England's most modern and vibrant football arenas.

Nestled in the Eastlands area of East Manchester, the stadium's distinctive elliptical roof and 53,400 capacity make it an iconic symbol of the club's ambition and growth. With the pitch positioned 6 metres below ground level, it was designed to be an intimate, intimidating gladiatorial arena.

Record Books

Key Achievements

The Treble: 2022–23
In the 2022–23 season, Manchester City became only the second English club in history to win the Treble — Premier League, FA Cup, and UEFA Champions League in a single season — cementing Pep Guardiola's side as one of the greatest teams ever assembled.
100 Points in a Season
In 2017/18, City became the first Premier League team to reach 100 points in a single season — a record that still stands.
Erling Haaland – Record Breaker
Haaland shattered Alan Shearer's long-standing Premier League record with 36 goals in his debut season — 2022/23.
21 Consecutive Wins
Set the English top-flight record with 21 consecutive wins across all competitions in the 2020/21 season.
Rodri – Ballon d'Or 2024
Rodri became the first Manchester City player to win the prestigious Ballon d'Or, capping a remarkable personal and team year.
4 Consecutive PL Titles
From 2020/21 to 2023/24, City won four Premier League titles in a row — the first club in English football history to achieve that feat.
FIFA Club World Cup 2023
Completed the Grand Slam by lifting the Club World Cup trophy in Saudi Arabia, becoming world champions for the first time.
